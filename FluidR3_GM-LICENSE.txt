===== This SF3 =====

FluidR3_GM.sf3 is the Debian fluid-soundfont-gm 3.1-6 FluidR3_GM.sf2
(sha256 74594e8f4250680adf590507a306655a299935343583256f3b722c48a1bc1cb0), every preset,
with its left/right sample links restored and each sample compressed to Ogg Vorbis
(libvorbis q0.4). Sample rates unchanged.
